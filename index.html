      Our Policy

      Decentralized · Encrypted · Unstoppable

      This is a decentralized app. Even the developer cannot access the contents exchanged between users.

      The existence of this app depends purely on its users. Everything is stored locally, therefore each and every user is a node that enhances the existence of this app.

      The app cannot be shut down in any way, since it is decentralized. The developer can only provide new updates to the same app.

      Feel secure. Feel secret. Share anything.

      What this means in practice
      • Messages and files are end‑to‑end encrypted in your browser before they leave your device.
      • There is no central server — the developer has nothing to hand over.
      • No accounts, no tracking, no logs. Refreshing clears your local session.
      • Your browser is the client and the node. Every copy of this page strengthens the network.
